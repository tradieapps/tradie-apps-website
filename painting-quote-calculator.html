How to price a job by area

Most painters, tilers and flooring installers quote labour as a rate per square metre (or square foot) for each type of surface: walls, ceilings, trims, floors. Multiply each area by its rate, add materials (often with a small markup to cover pick-up and handling), add any prep or call-out charge, then tax.

Your rate should cover your time, travel, consumables, insurance and the profit you need, so work it out from your own costs rather than copying someone else's. A quick check: divide the total by the area. If the effective price per m² looks too low for the prep involved, your rate probably is too.

Common lines on a painting quote

  • Walls, 2 coats
  • Ceilings, 2 coats
  • Doors and frames (often priced per item)
  • Skirting and architraves (per metre)
  • Prep: patching, sanding, sealing
