How much waste should I allow?

Cuts at walls, doorways and around obstacles mean you always need more material than the bare floor area. As a rule of thumb, allow about 5% for straight-laid planks, 10% for tiles laid square, 15% for diagonal layouts and up to 20% for herringbone or chevron. Add a little more for small, awkward rooms, and keep a few spare tiles for future repairs.

Example

A 4.2 m × 3.0 m kitchen is 12.6 m². With 10% waste that's 13.86 m². Tiles at 1.44 m² per box need 13.86 ÷ 1.44 = 9.6, so buy 10 boxes.

Tips

  • Buy all boxes from the same batch (check the shade/lot number).
  • Round up to whole boxes. Most suppliers will take back unopened boxes, but check first.
  • For walls, enter width × height of each wall as the extra area.
