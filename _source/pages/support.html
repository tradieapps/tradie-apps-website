Subscriptions

How do I cancel my subscription or free trial?

On your iPhone, open Settings → tap your name → Subscriptions → choose the app → Cancel Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the trial or period ends to avoid being charged.

I changed phones. How do I get Pro back?

Install the app with the same Apple ID, open the paywall and tap Restore Purchases.

Can I get a refund?

Apple handles all payments and refunds. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

QuoteWalk

The scan button doesn't appear

Room scanning needs an iPhone with LiDAR (iPhone 12 Pro or newer Pro models). Make sure camera access is allowed in Settings → QuoteWalk.

Tips for a better scan

Turn the lights on, move slowly, and point at every wall, door and window. Avoid mirrors and big glass areas where you can.

Invomile

Trips aren't being recorded

Turn on trips in the app, and check that location access is allowed in Settings → Invomile → Location (choose “Always” so trips are picked up while the app is closed).

I bought the old paid version

If you bought the earlier one-off paid version, Pro is unlocked for you for free. If it isn't, email me with the details.
